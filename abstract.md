**Abstract:**

When electronic devices malfunction, consumers often face significant challenges in finding trustworthy, local repair shops with transparent pricing. Traditional search engines lack specific repair estimates and real-time job tracking, leading to a frustrating user experience and lost business for skilled neighborhood technicians. 

Fixly addresses this gap by providing a dual-sided, hyper-local electronics repair marketplace. For consumers, Fixly offers a map-based discovery interface to locate nearby repair experts, view upfront estimated base costs, and track the repair lifecycle in real-time. For technicians, the platform provides a comprehensive triage dashboard to manage incoming requests, issue binding quotes, update job statuses, and build reputation through verified customer ratings. Built on a modern MERN stack with React 19 and Tailwind CSS v4, Fixly eliminates the friction of electronics repair, fostering trust and efficiency in the local repair economy.
